# Worked examples — see the flow before you risk your own meeting

Two complete runs, end to end: the messy meeting text that went in, and
everything the kit handed back. Nothing here is a screenshot and nothing is
summarized — this is the actual shape of the output.

Read them in order. They are the same fictional business, four months apart,
because the whole point of the kit only shows up on the second run.

| Folder | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `01-first-meeting/` | A first run from a phone voice memo. Minutes in the fixed format with a missing date flagged rather than invented, one action correctly marked Unassigned, and a fresh ledger. The aging report is nearly empty on purpose — this is what your day one looks like. |
| `02-fourth-meeting/` | The same business four meetings later. Same skeleton, same effort — but now the aging report names four overdue items, says who is carrying the most, and flags the commitment that has been re-promised in four straight meetings without closing. This is the run people pay for. |
| `03-refusal/` | The kit saying no. A thin status-update meeting trips the trackability gate, the kit stops and asks before writing anything, and the user's answer decides what happens next. |

**Vitale Landscaping & Masonry is invented.** So is every person in these
files. Your own run will have your people's names in it — that is the point,
and it is why the installer asks for your meeting text instead of demoing on
these.

## What to do with them

1. Read `01-first-meeting/` top to bottom. It takes about two minutes.
2. Open `02-fourth-meeting/aging-report.md` and read the "Keeps coming back"
   section. If you have ever thought "didn't we already talk about that?",
   that section is the kit.
3. Then paste your own last meeting into your AI and say "take minutes."

## A note on what the aging report is for

The report surfaces loose ends the meeting rhythm hides. It is not a
performance review and the kit does not write it as one. A commitment that has
been re-promised four times is almost never a lazy person — it is usually
blocked, badly defined, or sitting with someone who does not control the thing
it depends on. Surfacing it is how it gets taken OFF that person's plate.
