# OUTPUT — asking "what's overdue" with no meeting at all

This is the Monday-morning use, and it is the reason the kit gets opened on
days when nobody met. Sal typed two words into the same chat on Thursday,
August 13, 2026 - three days after the meeting above, with no transcript and
nothing pasted:

> **Sal:** what's overdue

The kit answered off the ledger it already has. No meeting text was needed
and none was asked for.

---

```
Two things are past their date:

- Vinny - the revised grading photos to Mrs. Kaplan, due July 24, now 20
  days ago.
- You - the commercial vehicle insurance renewal, due July 31, now 13 days
  ago. That one lapses rather than just slipping, so it's the one I'd do
  first.

Still open but not late: the Bohemia paperwork (due tomorrow, and it has
been re-promised four meetings running), the Selden proposal, and the F-350
booking for today.

Want me to draft a one-line note to Vinny about the photos?
```

---

These phrasings all reach the same report:

- "what's overdue"
- "who owes me what"
- "what's still open"
- "where do we stand"
- "what keeps slipping"
- "what did we decide about the price increase"

The last one is answered off the Decisions Made sections of the saved
minutes, not the ledger — "8% on new estimates only, effective September 1;
anything already quoted is honored."

## Why this matters more than it looks

Before this, the kit only did anything when a meeting ended: it was a
write-only archive. The standing question turns four months of accumulated
ledger into something you can pull on any morning in ten seconds. Same data,
already there, now answerable.
