## What the gate counts here

- **Owned actions: 0.** Nobody agreed to do anything.
- **Decisions: 0.** Nothing was settled.
- **Loose ends: 0.** Nothing was named as waiting, late, blocked, or missing —
  "quiet" and "all good here" are the opposite of a loose end.

Total: 0. That is under two, so the gate trips.

**Compare it to the near miss.** If Marisol had said "the Selden thing, still
waiting on them" and "I'll re-send the proposal I guess", that is one loose end
plus one owned action — a total of 2, and the kit would NOT stop. That is the
right call: an unanswered customer and a proposal going back out is real
material for the ledger, and the aging report will find them again in two weeks
whether or not anybody called it a decision. The gate is about whether there is
anything to track, not about whether the meeting was impressive.
